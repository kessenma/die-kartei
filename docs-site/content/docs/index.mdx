Die Kartei is a German tutor that runs on your own iPhone, iPad and Mac: flashcards with spaced
repetition, grammar drills, stories, listening practice and conversations, with its own language
model on device. No account, no server, no subscription.

<KarteiIllustration />

These docs are for the people building it. They cover how the app is put together and the work in
progress, starting with the Android port.
