## Job prep

<Screens>
  <Screen src="job-prep" alt="The Job prep room" caption="Interview practice, job ads, and your Lebenslauf." />
</Screens>

- **Interview practice** with an AI recruiter, for a real posting.
- **Study a job ad** line by line. The words you look up become the posting's own deck.
- **Lebenslauf**: your CV, so the recruiter asks about your career. It stays on the device.

## Deutschkurs

For a class you take elsewhere: a university course, an evening class, a tutor. Log what each
lesson covered, keep the handouts (read word by word), and track homework. Each course builds its
own deck from the words you log.
