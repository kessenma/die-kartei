title: Schreiben & Zeichnen

---

Three rooms for slower, hands-on practice. The Apple Pencil works in all three on iPad.

  <Screen src="zeichnen" alt="The Zeichnen room" caption="Zeichnen: draw your words." />
</Screens>

- **Schreiben.** A dated journal in German, English or both, and essays to a prompt. The tutor
  checks an essay sentence by sentence.
- **Schreibblatt.** The paper drill: pick words, choose how many times to write each, then fill
  the tables with the Pencil or print them. It's copying practice; nothing is graded.
- **Zeichnen.** Drawing a word helps you remember it. Quick sketches against the clock, slow
  studies in the Atelier, or paint by number. Every word gets a folder of its pictures.
