The core of the app is a card box. **Wortschatz** holds the Goethe-Institut A1–B1 word lists
(about 2,800 words) as one box. Your own decks sit next to it in the Library.

## Your own decks

<Screens>
  <Screen src="vocabulary" alt="The Vocabulary room" caption="Make cards on a topic, or from a document." />
  <Screen src="library" alt="The Library tab with decks" caption="Library: every deck. Mix decks to study several at once." />
</Screens>

A deck can come from a topic you type, a vocab sheet or handout (each row becomes a card), or a
phrase you save while reading. Cards can hold phrases as well as single words.
