## Lesen · Reading

<Screens>
  <Screen src="lesen" alt="The Reading room" caption="Stories, documents and photos of text." />
</Screens>

- **Short stories**, written at your level (A1–C1), read or listened to, then a quiz.
- **A paper or link**: import a PDF or web page and read it word by word.
- **Scan a photo** to pull out the German text.

In anything you read, double-tap a word to translate it, or select a phrase to save it as a card.

## Sprechen · Speaking

<Screens>
  <Screen src="sprechen" alt="Conversation Practice with saved conversations" caption="Conversations, out loud or typed." />
  <Screen src="phrasen" alt="The phrase library, empty" caption="Phrasen: phrases you heard but didn't understand." />
</Screens>

Pick a topic, a deck or a scenario (in the café, at the pharmacy) and talk. The tutor corrects you
as you go. **Stammtisch** mode makes it harder on purpose: topic changes, a reply timer, and
background noise.
