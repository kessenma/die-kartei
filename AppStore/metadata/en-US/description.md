Die Kartei is a German tutor that lives on your device. It runs its own custom-trained language model on iPhone, iPad, and Mac, so it works offline, making it more affordable than AI tutor apps relying on an external server, and never asks for a subscription.

/// a Model Trained for German, not Small Talk
German Tutor is fine-tuned on German instruction instead of general chat. The app checks how much memory your device has and recommends a model size. From there it writes your stories, runs your drills, and builds your cards, all on device. The model and its training data are open on Hugging Face (sources available in the settings screen of the app)

/// Conversations that Correct you
Order at a restaurant, sort out a problem at the bank, or practice for a job interview. The tutor answers in German with the English underneath. When you slip, it shows the fix and the reason, Take a hint, or say it in English and get the German back.

/// A Coach that Remembers your Mistakes
An entry placement quiz puts you somewhere from A1 to C1. After that, the app keeps track of what you get wrong in flashcards, stories, games, and conversations, and works those weak spots back into your practice. The streak calendar shows progress. Customize your own push notification practice reminders that won't pester you for a subscription.

/// Games Built from your Own Words
Vocab Matching races German against English using your own decks. Der/Die/Das is quick-fire article practice from an on-device dictionary. Prepositions drills with: Akkusativ, Dativ, Wechsel, and Genitiv, with Wo/Wohin examples that show you why.

/// Let it Work While you're Away
Line up stories, decks, and illustrations and let your device build them in the background with "Batch mode". Plan It for Me fills the queue after a few questions. Each job shows a time estimate, and you can close the app while they finish.

/// On Every Screen you Use
Die Kartei runs natively on MacOS, iOS, and iPadOS. Custom Apple Pencil exercises are built in and, the Die Kartei keyboard brings German corrections into other apps, and on Mac you can translate or correct selected text from the Services menu.
