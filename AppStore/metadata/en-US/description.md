A GERMAN MODEL, FINE-TUNED FOR THIS APP
German Tutor is trained specifically on German instruction rather than general chat. Pick the size that fits your phone, the app reads your available memory and recommends one, then it writes the stories, runs the drills, and builds your cards, all on device. Model and training data are open on Hugging Face.

A COACH THAT KNOWS WHAT YOU GET WRONG
An entrance quiz places you from A1 to C1. From there the app tracks the mistakes you make across flashcards, stories, games, and conversations, then works those weak spots back into your practice. Set a daily goal in minutes, watch your level and your pyramid fill in, and check the streak calendar for what you actually did. Reminders on whatever cadence you set, or none.

GAMES THAT USE YOUR OWN VOCABULARY
Vocab Matching pairs German and English against the clock using your own decks. Der/Die/Das is rapid article practice drawn from an on-device dictionary. Prepositions drills the one nobody masters: Akkusativ, Dativ, Wechsel, or Genitiv, with the Wohin/Wo examples that show you why.

QUEUE THE HEAVY WORK
Line up stories, decks, and illustrations and let your phone build them while you are away. Plan It for Me fills the queue from a few questions. Jobs run top to bottom with a time estimate, and you can leave the app while they finish.
